MARATHA HISTORY

Walk Through the History of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj

The Sahyadri mountains played a major role in the history of the Maratha kingdom. Hill forts served as important centres of administration, defence and strategic control.

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj captured, developed and used a network of forts across the region. These forts remain an important part of Maharashtra's historical and cultural heritage.

  • Raigad became the capital of the Maratha kingdom.
  • Torna was captured by Shivaji Maharaj in his early campaign.
  • Sinhagad is associated with the bravery of Tanaji Malusare.
  • The forts demonstrate remarkable military architecture and knowledge of the terrain.
